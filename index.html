Curated · Vintage 1928

The Lexington

Step upstairs and into another era. Layered in deep greens, wine reds, and warm golds, this intimate lounge draws inspiration from private clubs, classic whiskey rooms, and the timeless elegance of Kentucky racing culture. Designed for cocktails, private dinners, intimate celebrations, and distinctive gatherings, the space feels secluded, sophisticated, and entirely its own.

Furniture, décor, tabletop, glassware, staging, production elements and more are available for events both at La Jolla and at locations throughout South Florida. Whether you're hosting with us or bringing your event to another venue, our team can provide the elements and services needed to bring your vision together. All-inclusive weddings & milestone events · 2026–2027

Choose your Sapphire Collection

Two comprehensive ways to celebrate at La Jolla Coral Gables.

Sapphire Event Collection

A complete event package featuring the venue, partial planning, catering and premium open bar, staffing, rentals, florals, DJ and event lighting.

Weddings only

Sapphire Complete Story Collection

Everything in the Event Collection, plus a pre-wedding portrait session, full-day photography, a Full Story recap film and the Instant Memories Gallery.

Included in both collections

  • The house for a four-hour celebration
  • Partial planning and event coordination
  • Catering, premium open bar, service staff and bartenders
  • Tables, chairs, linens, place settings and event rentals
  • Florals for the ceremony or celebration, guest tables and featured moments
  • Professional DJ and MC services
  • Up-lighting and ambiance wall washes throughout the venue

The Complete Story difference

Created exclusively for weddings, the Complete Story Collection adds a pre-wedding engagement session, ten consecutive hours of wedding-day photography with two photographers, a professionally edited Full Story recap film, a mounted portrait, a private digital gallery and same-event access to selected photographs through the Instant Memories Gallery.

Photography and film can also be added to the Event Collection.

Guests
Up to 265, subject to the floor plan

The partner team

The Sapphire Collection is fulfilled by La Jolla’s trusted partner team: Epic Lux Management, Dade County Food Group, Altara, Design Zambrano, Twilight Production, Inc. & Abril Photography.

Good to know

Details to consider when selecting and scheduling services.

  • Event schedule. Coverage is based on consecutive contracted hours; time before or after them is arranged in advance.
  • Security and valet. Valet and event security are required, sized to the event type and guest count.
  • Holiday dates. December 31 and January 1 receive a custom proposal.
  • Gratuities. Gratuities are not included.
  • Availability. Services, partners, dates and products remain subject to availability until the agreement is signed.
