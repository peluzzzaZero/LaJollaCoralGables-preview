# v1.16.4 — coherent forest readers and editorial typography

Opening a reader while its chapter was fading in or out kept the entire reading panel at the caption's partial opacity. The owner's recording showed dark event-category text mixed with the piano/ballroom backdrop. Reproduction at chapter progress .343 left the open panel at 16% opacity; at the chapter centre it appeared correctly, which the previous tests covered.

An open reader is now fully opaque throughout the fade, with an opaque forest surface matching the film/header/footer and approved content. The desktop reader is a compact side panel; tablet/mobile readers use a single column. Cormorant Garamond titles restore an editorial character while Manrope keeps long copy and controls readable. Both fonts are self-hosted; the new 36 KB Latin/Spanish display subset includes its SIL licence. Closing restores the caption's cinematic fade. Video scrubbing, chapter positions, keyboard focus and the single Schedule a visit action remain in place.

Regression checks open all seven readers at both ends of their transitions, in EN/ES at 390, 884 and 1366px, in Chromium and WebKit. They check effective ancestor opacity, background, text contrast, reader scrolling, Escape/focus return and restoration of the closed fade. Contrast checks now reject translucent ancestor containers instead of treating their specified background as fully opaque.

Exact-head external review, complete CI, deployment and immutable tag evidence are recorded in the feature/release PRs. The uploaded recording and local frames remain outside the public repository. No new media, real email or booking submissions.
