# v1.32.0 — the owners' extra pictures: The Lexington, "Imagine it your way" and the floor plan

On 27 October 2026 the owners gave Dan a four-page PDF, "LA JOLLA pictures extra", to put on the site. Dan chose the placement in the project thread after three rounds of mock-ups ("me encanta.. adelante").

1. **The Lexington** shows the owners' picture of a dark panelled lounge with wine-red velvet armchairs and gold lamps (PDF page 4, right), in the existing arched frame, instead of the awning monogram drawing. The owners' copy is unchanged.
2. **"Imagine it your way" / "Imagínelo a su manera"**, a new board between the occasions and The Lexington, on the brand's sage-and-cream stripes. It holds the facade dressed as a tea house (page 3), the blush tea salon (page 4, left) and the oval welcome plaque with flowers (page 1; the same picture as `assets/brand/flower-plaque.jpg`, which had not been shown on the page since v1.28.0). From 901 px the facade and salon form a split diptych with the LJ seal in the middle and the plaque as a tilted polaroid on the corner; up to 900 px all three are polaroids.
3. The tour's **"Good to know"** card ends with the floor plan (page 2), first and second floor; it opens full size in a new tab. Every word, measurement, area, ceiling height and build note, the red and pink dimension lines and the logo are removed (the script fails if any text remains or the drawing touches the crop edge); nothing on the page states a size or capacity.

`scripts/extra-photos.py` rebuilds every image from the PDF (kept out of git in `media/extra/`; SHA-256 recorded) as AVIF with a WebP fallback, sRGB, no metadata; `--check` (run in CI) compares the committed files with `assets/venue/web/extra-photos.json`. The café menu board on page 3 shows prices and is always outside the crop. `tests/inspiration.test.mjs` checks placement, translations, the absence of prices, capacity, the banned room word and Facebook, and the records; the tour and Lexington tests follow the new pictures.

Verification before integration: `npm run build && npm test`, `python scripts/extra-photos.py --check` and the Chromium browser suites run locally; local screenshots of the board, The Lexington and the tour card in EN/ES at 320, 390, 768, 884, 901, 1024, 1200 and 1440 px. The external review (Claude CLI, Sonnet/high) and the CI results, including WebKit, are recorded on the pull requests, not here.

Provenance and limitations: the venue owners supplied the PDF to be published on the website; the pictures show La Jolla's own mark and the house's own "Maison de Thé" tea-salon styling, no third-party brand. The facade, salon and lounge pictures may be renderings rather than photographs; Dan was told and decided on 2026-10-27 that the page shows them without any "styling concept" label, whether they are photographs or not ("sean reales o no NO QUIERO QUE LLEVEN ESO"). The alt texts describe what each picture shows. The board images and the plan are single-size files (no `srcset`) of 54–154 KB each. The captions and the board title are ours, not owner copy. The source images are 1122 px (facade) to 3299 px; the facade crop is 660×825 and looks soft on high-density desktop screens. The PDF images are used as RGB without colour-profile conversion. The approval is Dan's (site owner), relaying the owners' request to publish the PDF.
