# v1.22.0 — reviews, location and a quiet credit

The owner was pleased with the mobile scenes of v1.21.0 and asked for: desktop polish of the entrance film and its frame, guest reviews and opinions integrated together with the location, and a discreet gray "powered by peluzzza" at the very bottom. During the cycle the client's stakeholder added three changes: remove the brand plates section in favour of an organised Instagram section with photos and reviews, never use the word "ballroom", and stop showing the bar in the spaces photograph.

1. Desktop entrance — the header is opaque on the landing, so from 651 px (tablet and desktop) the film, its shade and the thin frame now start below it. Before, the top 76 px of the approach film and the frame's top edge were hidden behind the header, so the frame looked open at the top. The frame, the arch scene and the arrival phrases keep their scroll behaviour; mobile is unchanged.
2. Client requirement: nothing on the site says or refers to "ballroom". Every visible text, alt text, map query and caption now says "hall" / "salón"; asset files, CSS classes and translation keys were renamed (`assets/venue/hall*.jpg|webp`, `assets/venue/cinematic/hall*`), the unused "Ballroom & catering" lockup was deleted and a static test fails if the word returns to a public page, script or asset name. The Facebook card and link (facebook.com/lajollaballroom) are removed: the owner confirmed that page is not La Jolla Coral Gables' account, and no Facebook link returns until the owner supplies the right one. Outside the site's control: the name of the Google listing, and the building's own sign visible in the facade photographs and the entrance film.
3. The spaces — at the stakeholder's request the photograph showing the bar is replaced by the piano photograph as the chapter's lead image ("The hall · a space to make your own"); the album's fourth print becomes the ivy facade on Alcazar Avenue so the piano is not shown twice.
4. From the house (replaces "A world of details") — the brand wall of seven identity plates is removed. In its place, after the services, a chapter on Instagram: six owner-delivered venue photographs (facade, street view, the hall with the piano, garden, staircase, music detail) as a 4:5 mosaic opening @lajollacoralgables, the plates' scatter-and-settle scroll scene now applied to these prints, then "Guest experiences" (the Google reviews component with links to read and write a review; no testimonial, star count or aggregate rating is written into the page), then the Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest profile cards. The team chapter no longer carries the social block.
5. Location — a chapter between the team and the invitation (also "Location" / "Ubicación" in the header): address, phone, email, Google directions, Apple Maps and a Google map that is a keyless embed created only after the visitor presses "Show the map". Until then a facade photograph stands in and no third-party request is made; the privacy page explains this in both languages.
6. Credit — "powered by peluzzza" in 10 px gray type, centered under the copyright line of the footer, identical in both languages and excluded from translation.

Also in this release (merged to develop separately in #77): the six-tile mosaic that shipped in v1.21.0 is now generated from `assets/social/tiles.json` with `python scripts/social-tiles.py` (6 or 12 tiles; owner originals stay git-ignored; post tiles need a recorded post approval and EN/ES labels; CI runs `--check`). This release uses it to switch the tiles to venue photographs (item 4).

Under 900 px the map frame has an explicit height: WebKit collapsed it to zero (its children are all positioned), which hid the control and the opened map in Safari. The browser test now clicks the control for real in Chromium and WebKit and checks the frame height.

No new video, generated media, scripts or trackers. The browser test intercepts the map request and the Places SDK; no real Google call is made in CI.

Release gate (results recorded in the feature and release pull requests): the static/content suite, the browser review in EN and ES at every reviewed width including the new social and location chapters and the footer, real Chromium/WebKit entrance decoding, landing fallbacks, desktop anchors and the mocked reviews/map cases. Exact-head external review, successful CI and visual inspection are required before each merge. Known limitations: the cloud review environment cannot reach google.com, so the embedded map itself was verified with an intercepted page rather than the live Google map; the credit's light gray is intentionally quieter than body text (about 5:1 at 10 px).

Next useful improvements: the owner's restricted Google browser key (Maps JavaScript API + Places API (New)) saved as the GOOGLE_MAPS_BROWSER_KEY Actions variable so selected reviews appear on the page, owner-chosen Instagram posts for the mosaic, and device testing on older iOS Safari.
