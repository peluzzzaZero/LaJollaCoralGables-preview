# v1.29.0 — a photograph for every occasion

The owner found the Occasions chapter monotonous: one photograph (the first dance) stood beside all ten occasions. They also asked for a distinctive hover on the "One hall, many moods" cards.

1. Each occasion has its own photograph from the owner's approved media (`tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md`): Weddings — first dance; Quinceañeras & Sweet Sixteens — the head table on the white floral dance floor; Social celebrations — the dessert table; Brand activations — the house lit at night; Film, photo & content — the spiral staircase; Corporate events — the holiday party; Productions & entertainment — the grand piano; Galas & fundraisers — lilac dressed tables; Private dining — the set table; Something completely different — the sheet music on red velvet.
2. From 1051px with the page script: a square sticky frame shows the photograph of the occasion being read, pointed at or focused, with the occasion title and ten progress ticks. Reading is followed in every motion mode. Full motion adds a rise from .3 opacity and, after 3.6 s on a two-card row, turns once to its second card (no indefinite rotation), only while the list is on screen and the tab is visible; reduced motion and SaveData switch instantly with no timer. A card left under a resting pointer stops steering once it scrolls out of the row being read, and the frame returns to that row; a card holding keyboard focus keeps the frame while the page scrolls and when the mouse leaves. The counter shows in every motion mode. The stacked slides load together when the list reaches the screen, except under SaveData. Only the shown slide is exposed to assistive technology (`aria-hidden="true"` on the others).
3. Up to 1050px, and at any width without the page script: each card carries its own photograph above its title.
4. "One hall, many moods": on hover the card lifts, the photograph eases closer, the mood's own light glows in from the edges (an animated registered custom property), the caption opens up with a line in that colour and the other moods desaturate. Touch screens show the coloured line without hover; reduced motion keeps the colour without movement.

Four new WebP delivery files (`assets/venue/web/occasion-*.webp`) are re-encodings of approved JPEGs with their framing unchanged; pixels are not retouched. Retired media (the bar photograph) is never used; a test checks it. Owner copy is unchanged. New EN/ES alt text avoids the banned word.

Tests: `tests/events.test.mjs` asserts ten distinct photographs in frame and cards, alt keys in both languages and the no-script fallback; the new `tests/browser/occasion-photos.py` (Chromium and WebKit in CI) checks reading-follow down and back up the list in full and reduced motion at 1280/1440, `aria-hidden="true"` on every hidden slide, hover hand-off, keyboard focus holding the frame while scrolling, the Spanish label, loaded wide-screen slides, the single turn in full motion and none under reduced motion, loaded per-card photographs at 320–1024 and the no-script layout; the static suite checks the recorded hashes of the new files.

Verification before the release pull request: `npm test` (148 passing); in local Chromium `occasion-photos.py`, `review.py`, `landing-motion.py`, `landing-flow.py`, `desktop-review.py` and `social.py` passed; WebKit runs in CI. Before/after screenshots at 390 and 1440 px in EN/ES (including the hover states and the frame at rows 01, 02·03, 04·05, 06·07, 08·09 and 10) were inspected; `occasion-photos.py` also lays out 320, 768, 884 and 1024 px and checks for horizontal overflow.

Review evidence (model, effort, exact head, findings and resolutions) is recorded in feature pull request #104 and its release pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: up to 1050px and without the page script the frame keeps only its caption ("The details set the tone.") above the illustrated cards, as the chapter's prose. Sources are about 800px wide, so the square frame is close to 1:1 at standard density and soft on high-density screens. The occasion-to-photograph pairing is an editorial choice from the venue's own photographs, not a statement that each photograph shows that event type; the owner can ask for a different pairing. No real-device (iPhone/iPad) check was possible from the build environment.

Next useful improvement: full-resolution originals of the event photographs, and a dedicated quinceañera photograph if the owner has one.
