# v1.29.0 — a photograph for every occasion

The owner found the Occasions chapter monotonous: one photograph (the first dance) stood beside all ten occasions. They also asked for a distinctive hover on the "One hall, many moods" cards.

1. Each occasion has its own photograph from the owner's approved media (`tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md`): Weddings — first dance; Quinceañeras & Sweet Sixteens — the head table on the white floral dance floor; Social celebrations — the dessert table; Brand activations — the empty hall; Film, photo & content — the spiral staircase; Corporate events — the holiday party; Productions & entertainment — the grand piano; Galas & fundraisers — lilac dressed tables; Private dining — the set table; Something completely different — the sheet music on red velvet.
2. From 1051px with the page script: a square sticky frame shows the photograph of the occasion being read, pointed at or focused, with the occasion title and ten progress ticks. Reading is followed in every motion mode. Full motion adds a rise from .3 opacity and alternates the two cards of a row every 3.6 s while the list is on screen and the tab is visible; reduced motion and SaveData switch instantly with no timer. Only the shown slide is exposed to assistive technology.
3. Up to 1050px, and at any width without the page script: each card carries its own photograph above its title.
4. "One hall, many moods": on hover the card lifts, the photograph eases closer, the mood's own light glows in from the edges (an animated registered custom property), the caption opens up with a line in that colour and the other moods desaturate. Touch screens show the coloured line without hover; reduced motion keeps the colour without movement.

Five new WebP delivery files (`assets/venue/web/occasion-*.webp`) are uncropped re-encodings of approved JPEGs; pixels are not retouched. Owner copy is unchanged. New EN/ES alt text avoids the banned word.

Tests: `tests/events.test.mjs` asserts ten distinct photographs in frame and cards, alt keys in both languages and the no-script fallback; the new `tests/browser/occasion-photos.py` (Chromium and WebKit in CI) checks reading-follow in full and reduced motion at 1280/1440, hover hand-off, no timer under reduced motion, per-card photographs at 320–1024 and the no-script layout.

Review evidence (model, effort, exact head, findings and resolutions) is recorded in the feature and release pull requests, not in this file.

Limitations: sources are about 800px wide, so the square frame is close to 1:1 at standard density and soft on high-density screens. The occasion-to-photograph pairing is an editorial choice from the venue's own photographs, not a statement that each photograph shows that event type; the owner can ask for a different pairing.

Next useful improvement: full-resolution originals of the event photographs, and a dedicated quinceañera photograph if the owner has one.
