# v1.29.0 — a photograph for every occasion

The owner found the Occasions chapter monotonous: one photograph (the first dance) stood beside all ten occasions. They also asked for a distinctive hover on the "One hall, many moods" cards.

1. Each occasion now has its own photograph from the owner's approved media (`tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md`): Weddings — first dance; Quinceañeras & Sweet Sixteens — the white floral dance floor; Social celebrations — the dessert table; Brand activations — the empty hall; Film, photo & content — the spiral staircase; Corporate events — the holiday party; Productions & entertainment — the grand piano; Galas & fundraisers — lilac dressed tables; Private dining — the set table; Something completely different — the sheet music on red velvet.
2. Wide screens: the sticky frame changes to the occasion being read, pointed at or focused. Two-card rows alternate every 3.6 s; the new photograph rises from .3 opacity and a slight scale. The frame shows the occasion title and ten progress ticks. Reduced motion and SaveData switch instantly and never alternate on a timer.
3. Up to 1050px: each card carries its own photograph above its title; the single frame is no longer shown there (its caption remains).
4. "One hall, many moods": on hover the card lifts, the photograph eases closer, the mood's own light glows in from the edges, the caption opens up with a line in that colour and the other moods desaturate. Touch screens show the coloured line without hover; reduced motion keeps the colour without movement.

Five new WebP delivery files (`assets/venue/web/occasion-*.webp`) are re-encodings of existing approved JPEGs; pixels are not retouched. Owner copy is unchanged. New alt text in EN/ES avoids the banned word.

Verification: static suite; `review.py`, `landing-motion.py`, `desktop-review.py`, `landing-flow.py` in Chromium locally; full CI incl. WebKit; before/after screenshots at 390 and 1440 (hover states included); external Sonnet/high review of the exact head.

Next useful improvement: full-resolution originals of the event photographs.
