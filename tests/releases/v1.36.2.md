# v1.36.2 — the seal no longer covers "Good to know" on phones

The weekly visual checks of 26 October, 2 and 9 November 2026 showed the floating "Schedule a visit" seal on a 390px phone covering the end of "A tour appointment does not reserve a space for an event" in the tour's "Good to know" notes.

1. `script.js`: the seal already slipped off the right edge while the map passed under it on narrow screens. The same rule now covers the "Good to know" title and notes (`.tour-paper > h3, .tour-paper > ul`). While they cross the seal's band it waits off the edge and comes back once they have passed; the notes' own calendar link offers the same visit meanwhile. Keyboard focus still brings it back. On desktops and wide tablets (about 790px and up) the notes never reach the seal's column, so nothing changes there; on narrower tablets the seal slips aside the same way. A block far above or below the seal no longer stops the others from being checked.
2. `tests/browser/public-foundation.py` steps through the notes in both languages at every width and fails if the seal covers the title or any note. `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` pins the rule.

Verification: `npm run build && npm test`; the Chromium browser suites locally (public-foundation, landing-flow, review, chat, footer, desktop). CI also runs WebKit. Screenshots before and after at 390, 884 and 1440 in EN/ES. The external review (model, effort, exact head) is recorded on the pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: the seal is away for the notes plus a margin before and after (a few hundred pixels of scroll on a phone); the notes' calendar link is the visit action meanwhile. Elsewhere on phones the seal still passes over text while scrolling, as any floating button does; it shrinks into its corner while scrolling down (v1.35). Only the notes, which are read at rest, are kept clear.
