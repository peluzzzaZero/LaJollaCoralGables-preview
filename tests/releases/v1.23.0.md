# v1.23.0 — your tour

The owner shared the client's brochure and asked to take its style, typography and ideas to improve the site, not change it. The owner then set the focus: the site's goal is the tour. Prices and commercial details will be handled separately, so nothing from the brochure that could imply a package, an inclusion, a partner, a capacity or a price appears on the page; only its idea and its look.

1. Your tour — a new chapter after the services (also "Tour" in the header). It opens with the brochure's lockup: "Come see the house" in spaced capitals over "Your tour" in script, a flourish and "The best way to imagine your occasion at La Jolla is in person". Two photographs from the brochure in double-ruled frames, three steps (walk the house, picture your occasion, talk with our team), a "Good to know" paper card (tours are by appointment; a tour appointment does not reserve a space; bring your ideas) and a closing invitation with the "Schedule your tour" button, which opens the same-page visit calendar. Writing to info@ is the secondary path.
2. Style — the brochure's green is the official logo sage (#5e7060). The beige base the owner chose stays; sage is the accent of the new chapter (script titles, flourish, roman step numerals, frames, button) on a light sage paper. No new font: Cormorant Garamond and MonteCarlo already match the brochure's Garamond and script.
3. Photographs — at the owner's request, two photographs from the brochure (a place card with white florals; calla lilies with candles; the others carry traces of the brochure's lace overlay) and its handmade paper texture behind the card. 800×1000 WebP in `assets/tour/`, with brochure page, treatment, owner approval and checksum recorded in `assets/tour/source.json`. Alt text describes them without claiming where they were taken, and a caption names them as table details from La Jolla's brochure. Guest names on two place cards in the calla photograph are blurred. The record keeps who approved them, when, and on what rights.
4. The retired bar photograph (`assets/venue/hall.jpg`) and its unused films are no longer copied to the published site (`retiredMedia` in `scripts/build-site.mjs`).

5. Review follow-ups from v1.22 — the location map is the v1.22.1 Google Maps link (no embed); the no-embed test covers the Instagram and tour chapters again; old `#gallery` links land on the Instagram chapter; the README's content requirements match the site; `/media/` is ignored only at the repository root.

A static test fails if prices, packages, collections, inclusions, capacity, partners or the word "ballroom" appear in the chapter or its EN/ES copy.

No new video, generated media, scripts or trackers.

6. Smaller fixes from review — the mobile chapter menu scrolls inside short or landscape screens now that it has seven entries, and the tour steps keep list semantics in Safari/VoiceOver.

Release gate (results recorded in the feature and release pull requests): the static/content suite, the browser review in EN and ES at every reviewed width including the new chapter, desktop header fit, an external Sonnet/high review of the exact head, and mobile and desktop screenshots.

Limitations: the brochure does not say where its photographs were taken, so the page presents them as brochure details, not as rooms of the house. The tour button says "Schedule your tour" because the owner made the tour the site's focus; the header keeps "Schedule a visit". The paper texture behind "Good to know" is a CSS background and loads with the page.

Next useful improvement: approved high-resolution photographs of the house itself for the tour chapter, and the assistant the owner described, once its brief arrives.
