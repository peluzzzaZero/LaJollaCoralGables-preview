# v1.23.0 — the Sapphire Collection

The owner shared the client's "the Sapphire Collection" brochure (2026–2027, all-inclusive wedding and milestone event packages) and asked to take its style, typography and information into the site, without its prices, which are not confirmed, and to improve the page rather than change it.

1. The Sapphire Collection — a new chapter after the services (also "Collections" / "Colecciones" in the header). It opens with the brochure's lockup: "Choose your" in spaced capitals over "Sapphire Collection" in script, a flourish and "Two comprehensive ways to celebrate". It presents the Event Collection and the wedding-only Complete Story Collection side by side, what both include, the Complete Story difference, the house facts (guests described without a capacity figure, at the owner's request; a four-hour celebration with three hours to set up and one to close), the partner team named in the brochure and a "Good to know" paper card (schedule, security and valet, holiday dates, gratuities, availability). Pricing reads "shared at your private tour, by date and guest count".
2. Style — the brochure's green is the official logo sage (#5e7060). The beige base the owner chose stays; the sage is used as the accent of the new chapter (script titles, flourish, dots, rules, its button) on a light sage paper. No new font: Cormorant Garamond and MonteCarlo already match the brochure's Garamond and script.
3. Photographs — at the owner's request, five photographs from the brochure (a dressed guest table, the bride with the vintage car, candlelit glassware, calla lilies with candles, a floral reception) appear on the two collections and in a framed three-photo band; its handmade paper texture backs the "Good to know" card. Delivered as 800×1000 WebP in `assets/collection/`.
4. The tour comes first — the owner's main goal for the site is the private tour. The chapter ends with "Come see it in person" and a "Schedule a private tour" button that opens the same-page visit calendar; requesting details by email is a secondary link.
5. Excluded on purpose — every price, deposit, fee and "valued at" figure; the guest capacity figure (the owner prefers not to state it); the brochure's one use of the word "ballroom".
6. The retired bar photograph (`assets/venue/hall.jpg`) and its unused films are no longer copied to the published site (`retiredMedia` in `scripts/build-site.mjs`).

A static test fails if any currency, percentage or "valued at" figure, or the word "ballroom", appears in the chapter or its EN/ES copy.

No new video, generated media, scripts or trackers.

Release gate (results recorded in the feature and release pull requests): the static/content suite, the browser review in EN and ES at every reviewed width including the new chapter, desktop header fit, an external Sonnet/high review of the exact head, and mobile and desktop screenshots.
