# v1.32.1 — the "Imagine it your way" board comes alive

Dan saw v1.32.0 live and liked the board but found it static ("esta bonito si y me gusta pero estatico... ponle como algun efecto que combine con el sitio y la imagen").

1. **Arrival with the scroll**, driven by the board's own `--board-arrival` (set by `script.js` like the other scroll scenes: 0 as the board enters the view, 1 once its middle reaches the middle of the view), so it plays forwards and backwards. From 901 px the split closes: the facade slides in from the left and the salon from the right with a slight tilt, the LJ seal stamps the seam (it grows and turns into place) and the plaque polaroid drops onto the corner. Up to 900 px the three polaroids are dealt onto the card from three directions.
2. **Depth**: the facade and the salon drift gently inside their frames, like the history and hall photographs (`data-landing-parallax`).
3. **On a desktop pointer**: the picture under the pointer zooms slightly while the other one softens, its label lifts, the seal turns once, and the plaque straightens and comes forward.

Nothing moves under reduced motion or Save-Data (all arrival rules sit under `.landing-effects`; hover effects need `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`). No `opacity: 0` and no `clip-path`; pictures, text and labels are always visible. No content, image or copy changed.

Checks: `npm test`, `python scripts/extra-photos.py --check` and the browser suites, run locally on Chromium; screenshots at 1440 and 390 px in mid-arrival and settled poses.
