# v1.36.0 — the privacy and terms pages describe the chat's providers in general terms

Dan, 29 Oct 2026: the privacy page should not name where the chat's AI models or services come from; keep it general but accurate.

1. **Privacy, chat assistant (EN/ES).** The section no longer names the AI, hosting, security-check or email services. It says the messages, with the part of the page the chat was opened from, are processed by third-party technology and cloud service providers working for La Jolla, and that depending on the provider the text may be kept or used under that provider's own terms, including to operate and improve its services. It keeps the AI disclosure, what is sent (messages; name, phone or email only if the visitor leaves them), the 30-day deletion, the automated once-per-conversation person check, and the team email. "Phone numbers and email addresses are removed" becomes "we take steps to remove". New: La Jolla does not sell chat information or use it for advertising; the assistant can make mistakes; the contact section covers questions about the chat and deletion requests.
2. **Terms, new "Chat assistant" section (EN/ES).** Replies are generated automatically and may be incomplete or wrong; nothing in the chat is an offer, a binding quote or an event contract, and the chat does not reserve a date for an event.
3. Microsoft Bookings and Google stay named: visitors see them when they use them, and their own terms are linked.
4. `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` checks the new wording and that neither legal page names the chat's technology providers. `chat.js` is deliberately unchanged: its notice already says "AI services", and it still loads the person-check script from that service's own address in the browser, which the privacy text covers in general terms. The privacy page now shows its last-updated date (29 October 2026).

Verification: `npm run build && npm test`; screenshots of the chat sections of both legal pages at 320, 390, 884 and 1440 px in EN/ES, shared with the owner outside the repo. The external review evidence lives on the PR.

Limitations: "does not sell or use for advertising" was confirmed by Dan (29 Oct). This is not legal advice; the owners' lawyer should review the final policy. Earlier release notes in this repository still name the providers as history.
