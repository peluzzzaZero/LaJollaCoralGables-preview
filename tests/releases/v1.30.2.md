# v1.30.2 — the visit seal steps aside from the map and morphs with the scroll

After v1.30.0 the owner reported two problems from an Android phone: passing the Location map, the floating seal sat in the middle of the map, over the pin; and the change from the arrival button to the seal (and from the seal into "Let's begin") looked abrupt.

1. Map: while the map block (the Google map and its "Open in Google Maps" link) crosses the seal's corner, the seal slides off the right edge before the map reaches it and slides back once the map has fully passed. On wide screens with room beside the map (for example 1440 px), it instead waits in the margin to the right of the map. Focusing the link with the keyboard brings it back into view. Under reduced motion it moves out and back instantly. The previous behaviour (centred just above the map's bottom edge on narrow screens) is removed.
2. Transition: size, corners and position now follow the scroll frame by frame instead of switching state halfway with a timed CSS morph. Over the first ~45% of a screen the arrival button narrows into the round seal while travelling to its corner. Its words fade out over the first 40% of the way, and the monogram with its ring fades and grows in at the centre from 20% to 65%, always sized to the current height so nothing is cut off. Over the last ~35% of a screen before "Let's begin" the same morph runs in reverse into that section's button. Only the colours still ease (0.35 s) when the state changes. The 3 s heartbeat with 9 s rest, the single link, its accessible name, the focus ring, the no-script seal and the script-failure fallback are unchanged.

Tests: `tests/browser/public-foundation.py` now scrolls through the map block at every width and language and fails if the seal overlaps it anywhere in the viewport, and checks that keyboard focus brings a tucked seal back; `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` follows the new reduced-motion rounding.

Verification before the release pull request: `npm test` and the local Chromium browser suites listed in the pull request; scripted frame captures at 390×844 and 1440×900 (arrival → seal, through the map, into "Let's begin") were inspected and shared with the owner as short videos. WebKit runs in CI.

Review evidence is recorded in the feature pull request and its release pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: the Google map embed is blocked in the local captures, so the real map tiles were not seen, only the map block's box. The seal is repositioned from scroll events, so on touch devices it may trail native scrolling by a frame. No real-device check from the build environment.
