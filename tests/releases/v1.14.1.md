# La Jolla v1.14.1

Fix chapter 05 on mobile: its generated portrait ballroom take introduced ceiling distortion and retiming spread a fraction of its movement over several chapters. The landing now uses five-second excerpts of the owner's recorded facade films, with portrait centre crops of the same real frames. The generated ballroom and optical-flow portrait are removed from the landing; original room photographs remain available without architectural changes.

Chapter 05 has its own camera interval and traverses more than three seconds of footage during its reading band. The following chapters use a second recorded take, a return along the first take and the house shot, preserving the same continuous scroll experience, layered photos and reading choreography adapted from Scroll World and Elaya. The closing house interval remains continuous. No new generation, spend, audio, production DNS or GoDaddy Live publishing.

Delivery is H.264/MP4 and VP9/WebM with short GOPs, faststart, lazy loading and real-frame posters. Provenance, exact source offsets and hashes are recorded in `assets/venue/cinematic/chapter-five-source.json`. Browser checks exercise chapter 05 at the beginning and end of its reading band, confirming a decoded portrait video and visible movement, plus all chapter navigation, both languages and media failure/reduced-motion alternatives. These exterior films present the venue setting; they do not assert a filmed interior identity for The Lexington.

Mobile startup fix: WebKit could run the preparation playback to the end while a seek was pending. Priming now applies once per loaded blob, never starts an empty element, pauses on the first playing event with a 120 ms fallback, and restores the latest scroll frame after pausing. Metadata-ready seeking breaks any dependency on first-frame autoplay. Cleanup cancels priming timers/listeners. WebKit regression checks require changing decoded pixels, correct chapter-05 time and stable paused time during reading.
