# v1.34.0 — the visit seal opens La Jolla's chat assistant

Dan asked for the website chat first, working perfectly before Telegram, Instagram and WhatsApp ("antes que nada vamos a enfocarnos en la pagina web"). The owners' assistant guide (Epic Lux, "Guía de automatización: email y chat", 20 Oct 2026) is the source of truth, with the nuances Dan agreed: Microsoft Bookings rather than Google Calendar, tours 11 am to 5 pm Eastern, no Facebook, and the banned word. Dan approved the seal opening the chat, plus a fixed "Schedule directly" button inside it ("perfecto me encanta.. adelante").

## What changes

1. **The "Schedule a visit" seal opens the assistant** (guide, section 3A). The chat opens at the bottom right. It shows the guide's welcome with five buttons: plan an event, schedule a tour, services, become a vendor, talk to a person.
2. **"Schedule directly" / "Agendar directo"** is a fixed button at the top of the chat. It opens the same in-page Microsoft Bookings window as before, so a visitor who only wants the calendar is one more tap away. "Talk to a person" sits beside it.
3. **Fallback**:
   - If `chat.js` does not load (blocked, offline), the seal opens the calendar directly, as in v1.33.0.
   - Without JavaScript the seal is still the Bookings link.
   - If the assistant does not answer, the chat offers the calendar link.
4. **`chat.js`** is a copy of the assistant's widget (repository `lajollaAsistente`, `web-widget/widget.js`), served from this site with its own cache key. It talks to the assistant on Cloudflare Workers, which only accepts requests from lajollacoralgables.com.
5. **Privacy**: a new "Chat assistant" / "Asistente de chat" section covers:
   - The conversation is kept by the assistant service (Cloudflare) and in the browser for 30 days without activity.
   - Answers use an AI model run by Cloudflare, which never receives phone numbers or emails.
   - Details a visitor chooses to leave go to the team by email (Microsoft 365).
   - Visitors should not share sensitive information.
   - Estimates are informational; final pricing and terms are confirmed in writing.

## The assistant

- **Rules first.** Prices, the calendar, capacity and the banned words come from fixed rules and the owners' rate table. An AI model on Cloudflare (Workers AI, free allocation) only understands free text and writes answers from the approved cards. Phone numbers and emails are removed before any text reaches it. Other free models (NVIDIA, Gemini, Groq) take over if it fails, but only once their keys are added; the privacy page must be updated before that.
- **Estimates come only from the owners' rate table, under human control:**
  - The owners send changes and the table is updated.
  - With no changes, the last values apply.
  - Each estimate ends with an informational-estimate note.
- **Always a next step.** Every reply ends with suggestion buttons. The chat invites visitors to the tour or to call, with no invented urgency or offers. "Start over" / "Empezar de nuevo" in the header begins a new conversation.
- **Hand-off (guide, section 4).** A visitor can leave a name, a phone or email, the question and a good time. Questions the chat cannot answer are recorded. The team gets an email at info@ at once for a contact request, plus a summary during the Florida day only when there is something new. This starts once the Microsoft 365 email app is registered; until then requests are kept for the team page.
- **Cards.** The knowledge cards repeat the published website and the owners' guide; Dan approved them on 27 Oct 2026, and the owners can still ask for changes.
