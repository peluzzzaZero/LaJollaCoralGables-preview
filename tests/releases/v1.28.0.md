# v1.28.0 — the house dressed for events

The site owner (Dan) shared recent photographs of La Jolla dressed for real events and asked that the older images be replaced where the new ones fit best. Until now the landing showed the house mostly empty and two brand stationery pieces; these photographs show what the house becomes on the day.

1. "A space for every vision": a couple's first dance on the starlit floor sits beside Weddings (01), replacing the card among hydrangeas.
2. "At La Jolla & beyond": a real set table (glassware, gold chargers, white flowers, candle) replaces the welcome-table stationery; caption "Tabletop, glassware and florals" / "Mesa, cristalería y flores".
3. The album keeps the owners' captions, the staircase and the music score; the ivy tower with magenta bougainvillea now illustrates "Architecture and garden beauty" and the house at night in warm string lights illustrates "A Coral Gables landmark".
4. New "One hall, many moods" / "Un salón, muchos ambientes" strip after the occasions list: the same hall in ivory, amethyst, amber, coral and emerald light (five cards in a row on desktop, a swipeable row on phones).
5. "From the house" grows from 6 to 12 tiles: the white reception, the dessert table, the pink-dressed tables, a first dance, a toast on the dance floor, the couple on the balcony and a holiday party join the house photographs.

All alt texts are in English and Spanish. Dan confirmed the couple's permission, asked that every photograph of the delivery be used, and confirmed that all of them, including background guests and a client floor monogram, are the venue's promotional material (20 October 2026; see the clearance record in `tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md`). All sixteen are on the page. Provenance, hashes and crops are in `tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md`. The photographs arrived at about 800 px wide. Album, services and occasion images are delivered at their source size or smaller; the event mosaic tiles are 4:5 crops about 430 px wide, enlarged to 640×800 by `scripts/social-tiles.py` (it prints that warning), so they are soft on high-density screens until full-resolution originals replace them. The retired brand pieces (`welcome-table.jpg`, `mood-card-flowers.jpg`) stay in `assets/` for provenance, like earlier retired assets.

Verification: `npm test` (the MySQL and mail suites run in CI only); local Chromium screenshots of the occasions, services, album, moods strip and mosaic in EN/ES at 1920, 884 and 390, plus the moods strip at 320 without horizontal page overflow; local `tests/browser/landing-flow.py` (Chromium, 320 and 884, all six modes); full CI (Chromium and WebKit) on the feature pull request. The external Sonnet/high reviews, their heads and resolutions are recorded in #98.
