# v1.28.0 — the house dressed for events

The owner shared recent photographs of La Jolla dressed for real events and asked that the older images be replaced where the new ones fit best. Until now the landing showed the house mostly empty and two brand stationery pieces; these photographs show what the house becomes on the day.

1. "A space for every vision": a couple's first dance on the starlit floor sits beside Weddings (01), replacing the card among hydrangeas.
2. "At La Jolla & beyond": a real set table (glassware, gold chargers, white flowers, candle) replaces the welcome-table stationery; caption "Tabletop, glassware and florals" / "Mesa, cristalería y flores".
3. The album keeps the staircase and now shows the couple on the balcony ("The balcony" / "El balcón"), the ivy tower with magenta bougainvillea ("Garden light") and the house at night in warm string lights ("The house on Alcazar Avenue").
4. New "One hall, many moods" / "Un salón, muchos ambientes" strip after the occasions list: the same hall in ivory, amethyst, amber, coral and emerald light (five cards in a row on desktop, a swipeable row on phones).
5. "From the house" grows from 6 to 12 tiles: the white reception, the dessert table, the pink-dressed tables, a first dance, a toast on the dance floor and a holiday party join the house photographs.

All alt texts are in English and Spanish. The owner confirmed the couple's permission and asked that every photograph of the delivery be used (20 October 2026); all sixteen are on the page. Provenance, hashes and crops are in `tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md`. The photographs arrived at about 800 px wide, which covers the sizes shown; larger originals would sharpen them on high-density screens.
