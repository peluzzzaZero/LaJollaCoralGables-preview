# v1.34.1: the chat checks once that a person is writing

Dan asked for protection against bots, prompt injection, and anyone spending the assistant's free AI quota ("imagina que tienes a una persona tratando de gastar los tokens…"). He approved Cloudflare Turnstile on 29 Oct 2026 ("me gusta la idea de esa proteccion").

## What changes

1. **`chat.js`** is the assistant's widget, copied byte for byte from repository `lajollaAsistente`, `web-widget/widget.js`, commit 5976f4a. `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` pins it by SHA-256. When the assistant answers a message with `verification_required`, the widget does the following:
   - It loads Cloudflare Turnstile from `challenges.cloudflare.com`, and only then.
   - It runs the check inside the chat in the visitor's language. The check is invisible (`interaction-only`) and takes no room in the chat.
   - It sends the message again with the one-time token.
   - The check happens once per conversation. Later messages need no token.
   - The 20-second request limit still covers loading Turnstile and the invisible check.
   - If the check needs a tap, the box appears (compact on phones), and the limit pauses until the visitor taps it.
   - A check that fails, expires, times out, cannot run in the browser, or never loads ends with the usual help and the calendar link.
   - "Start over" removes a check in progress. A late answer from the old check does not affect the new conversation.
2. **Privacy** (EN/ES): Cloudflare may process network information to check, through Turnstile, once per conversation, that the chat is used by a person. The check usually needs no action.

Nothing changes for visitors until the assistant turns the check on, after this release is live on Pages. Before that, a cached page with an older `chat.js` would show the error help if the assistant asked for the check. Turning it on is therefore left until the Pages cache has refreshed.

## The assistant (repository `lajollaAsistente`, branch `feature/cloudflare-web-chat`; reported from there)

These changes are not part of this repository. Commits are listed so they can be checked there.

- eda9482:
  - Courtesy messages ("thanks", "ok", "bye", and their Spanish equivalents) get a warm fixed answer without an AI model, and an unfinished question is asked again.
  - Prompt-injection attempts get a fixed answer.
  - At most 40 messages a minute per network address.
  - A daily AI budget: 40 model calls per conversation and 3000 for the whole assistant.
  - Calls without the website's Origin are refused.
- db182e5 and 5976f4a: the Turnstile check on the server (once per conversation, stored in D1) and in the widget.
- aefd28c, the owners' test of 29 Oct: after an estimate, questions such as "what does that price include?" and "does it include food?" restarted the estimate. They are now answered. The chat never asks the same question a third time: after a "no", a question back or a second miss, it offers a tour or a call.
- 77bae20: the automatic evaluation grows from 54 to 102 conversations, written section by section from the owners' guide:
  - its golden rules: one question per message, no robotic phrases, at most one exclamation mark;
  - each flow from 5.1 to 5.13;
  - the rules and fees of section 7;
  - attacks: prompt injection, impersonating the manager, role play for a discount, markup, very long input, repeated messages;
  - conversations that change topic.

  New fixed answers cover the section 7 rules, The Lexington and the café (never an invented price or capacity), custom budgets, complaints, contracts and the social links.
- Results:
  - Unit tests: 181 pass.
  - Local evaluation: 102/102.
  - Live evaluation: 50/50 on Worker version e17d0b86 (eda9482) and 54/54 on 252ccebf (aefd28c). Results are on the `eval-results` branch.
