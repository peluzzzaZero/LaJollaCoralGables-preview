# v1.34.1: the chat checks once that a person is writing

Dan asked for protection against someone spending the assistant's free AI quota, bots and prompt injection ("imagina que tienes a una persona tratando de gastar los tokens…"). He approved Cloudflare Turnstile on 29 Oct 2026 ("me gusta la idea de esa proteccion").

## What changes

1. **`chat.js`** is the assistant's widget at commit db182e5 (repository `lajollaAsistente`). When the assistant answers a message with `verification_required`, the widget:
   - loads Cloudflare Turnstile from `challenges.cloudflare.com`, only then;
   - runs it inside the chat, usually invisibly (`interaction-only`, in the visitor's language);
   - sends the message again with the one-time token.
   - The check happens once per conversation, and later messages need no token.
   - While the check is on screen, the 20-second request limit pauses, so a visitor who has to tap the box is not cut off.
   - If the check fails, the chat shows the usual help with the calendar link.
2. **Privacy** (EN/ES): Cloudflare may process network information to check, through Turnstile, once per conversation, that the chat is used by a person. The check usually needs no action.

Nothing changes for visitors until the assistant turns the check on, which happens after this release is live. Older copies of `chat.js` would show an error if the assistant asked for the check.

## The assistant (same day, repository `lajollaAsistente`)

- Courtesy messages ("thanks", "ok", "bye", and Spanish equivalents) get a warm fixed answer without an AI model, and an unfinished question is asked again.
- Prompt-injection attempts get a fixed answer without an AI model.
- At most 40 messages a minute per network address.
- A daily AI budget: 40 model calls per conversation and 3000 for the whole assistant. Past a budget, the chat keeps answering from its fixed rules and cards.
- Calls without the website's Origin are refused.
- The owners' test on 29 Oct: after an estimate, "what does that price include?" and "does it include food?" restarted the estimate and repeated the date question. Now they get what the venue rental covers and what is separate (catering, bar, florals), and the chat never asks the same question again: after a "no", a question back or a second miss, it apologizes and offers a tour or a call.
- The protections went live on Cloudflare (version e17d0b86), and the live evaluation passed 50/50.
- The automatic evaluation now has 102 conversations, written section by section from the owners' guide: its golden rules (one question per message, no robotic phrases), each flow from 5.1 to 5.13, the rules and fees of section 7, and attacks (prompt injection, impersonating the manager, role play for a discount, markup, very long input, repeated messages). Rules and fees from section 7, The Lexington and the café (no invented price or capacity), custom budgets and the social links now have fixed answers.
