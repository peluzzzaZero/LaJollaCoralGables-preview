# v1.30.0 — one Schedule a visit that travels with the page

The owner found the landing repetitive: "Schedule a visit" appeared in the header, the arrival, under each of the ten occasions, after the spaces, after the hall moods, in the tour and in "Let's begin". They asked for a single button that starts at the top and, with the scroll, becomes a floating button that stays visible to the bottom of the page. A first version floated as a large pill; the owner found it took too much space and asked for something small on the right: a mini seal mixing the logo with "book".

1. The landing has exactly one "Schedule a visit" link (`#visit-cta`, EN/ES, same Microsoft Bookings calendar dialog as before). It rests in three places, each marked by an invisible slot: a button in the arrival (cream, where the old hero button was), a small round seal at the bottom-right while reading (64px, 76px from 901px, above the safe-area inset), and a button in "Let's begin" above the footer. The seal is the site's own LJ oval monogram (`assets/brand/logo-lj-oval-sage.png`, unchanged) ringed by "Book a visit · La Jolla" / "Reserve su visita · La Jolla" (SVG text on a circle, key `cta.seal`) on a translucent paper veil with a thin brown ring; on hover or focus the ring turns slightly. The visible label "Schedule a visit" / "Agende una visita" stays the link's accessible name (clipped, not hidden with zero opacity); the seal is `aria-hidden`.
2. With the page script the link is fixed and `initVisitCta()` moves it with `transform` only: over the first ~40% of a screen it travels from the arrival to the seal's corner (eased) and, halfway, shrinks from the button into the seal; over the last 30% of a screen before "Let's begin" it glides into that section and then scrolls with it, so it never covers the footer, the legal row or the language selector. Under reduced motion it changes place instantly with no easing or colour transition. While it floats, the arrival's "Discover the house" hint (which sat in the same corner) is hidden. When the Location map reaches the seal's band, the seal moves into the margin beside the map when there is room, or sits centred just above the map's bottom edge on narrow screens, so Google's controls, attribution and "Open in Google Maps" stay uncovered. Its position is recomputed on scroll, resize, language change and when late content resizes the page.
3. Removed: the header button, the ten per-occasion links, the button after the venue walk, "Let's imagine your occasion here" after the album, the button after "One hall, many moods" and "Schedule your tour" in the tour (and their unused copy keys `visit.cta`, `tour.cta`). Navigation links, the owners' invitation line ("Experience La Jolla in person…"), the tour's email path, "Inquire about The Lexington", and the planning, privacy and terms pages are unchanged.
4. With the script it stays unseen until it has been placed, so a cold load never shows the seal morphing into the arrival button (if the script never runs it appears after 3 seconds), and the monogram is fetched with the page rather than on first float. Without the page script the link is simply the seal at the bottom-right, the empty slots are not shown and the footer gains bottom padding so the legal row stays clear (also if the script fails before placing it; `initVisitCta()` runs right after the header, before the heavier scroll scenes). The button is at least 48px tall and the seal 64px, keyboard-focusable with a visible focus ring, hidden while the booking dialog is open and not printed. It comes right after the header in the tab order: keyboard and screen-reader users reach it first, while it is drawn in the arrival, the bottom or "Let's begin" — an accepted difference between reading order and position for a persistent call to action.

Occasion cards no longer contain a link, so the v1.29.0 behaviour "a card holding keyboard focus keeps the frame" no longer applies and its focus handlers are removed; pointer and reading-follow behaviour are unchanged. Unused `.inquire-link` landing rules and the removed buttons' CSS are deleted.

Tests: new `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` (one visit link, its three slots, touch size, reduced motion, dialog). Updated `opening`, `tour`, `events`, `scenes` (their slices ended at removed markup) and the browser suites (`public-foundation`, `review`, `landing-motion`, `immersive`, `occasion-photos`) to use the single button: it stays in the viewport and at least 44px tall, floats as the seal mid-page, is docked (`is-end`) above the footer at the end, floats again on the way back, has no transition under reduced motion, and occasion cards carry no links.

Verification before the release pull request: `npm test` (151 passing); in local Chromium `review.py`, `public-foundation.py`, `landing-motion.py`, `immersive.py`, `occasion-photos.py`, `desktop-review.py`, `footer-review.py`, `landing-flow.py`, `social.py`, `venue-walk.py` and `motion-quality.py` passed; WebKit runs in CI. A dedicated scripted pass (screenshots plus measured position and state) covered 320×640, 390×844, 768×1024, 884×884, 901×900 and 1440×900 in EN and ES: arrival, mid-transition, seal, middle, Location map, end, a fast reverse scrub back up and a return to the top, then keyboard Tab to the link. In all 99 measured frames the link stayed inside the viewport and never overlapped the footer; the states were start → float → end → float → start as expected; it is the first Tab stop after the header controls and shows its focus ring. The Spanish ring text renders without collisions at 64 and 76px. Under reduced motion at 390px it changes place instantly with zero transition duration. Before/after screenshots at 390 and 1440px were also inspected.

Review evidence (model, effort, exact head, findings and resolutions) is recorded in the feature pull request and its release pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: no tracking existed for the old buttons, so none was added. The button is repositioned from scroll events, so on touch devices it may trail native scrolling by a frame during its two short transitions; the Google map embed is blocked in the local screenshots, so its exact control layout around the centred seal on narrow screens was not seen. On very short phones the very bottom of the footer scrolls the docked button away with "Let's begin" (it is never drawn over the footer). No real-device (iPhone/iPad) check was possible from the build environment.

Next useful improvement: measure tour bookings from the single button once the owner chooses an analytics tool.
