# v1.30.0 — one Schedule a visit that travels with the page

The owner found the landing repetitive: "Schedule a visit" appeared in the header, the arrival, under each of the ten occasions, after the spaces, after the hall moods, in the tour and in "Let's begin". They asked for a single button that starts at the top and, with the scroll, becomes a floating button that stays visible to the bottom of the page.

1. The landing has exactly one "Schedule a visit" link (`#visit-cta`, EN/ES, same Microsoft Bookings calendar dialog as before). It rests in three places, each marked by an invisible slot of the same shape: the arrival (cream, where the old hero button was), a floating pill at the bottom (centred up to 700px, bottom-right on wider screens, above the safe-area inset), and "Let's begin" above the footer.
2. With the page script the link is fixed and `initVisitCta()` moves it with `transform` only: over the first ~40% of a screen it travels from the arrival to the pill (eased), the colour turns from cream to brown and the corners round; over the last 30% of a screen before "Let's begin" it glides into that section and then scrolls with it, so it never covers the footer, the legal row or the language selector. Under reduced motion it changes place instantly with no easing or colour transition. While it floats, the arrival's "Discover the house" hint (which sat in the same corner) is hidden.
3. Removed: the header button, the ten per-occasion links, the button after the venue walk, "Let's imagine your occasion here" after the album, the button after "One hall, many moods" and "Schedule your tour" in the tour (and their unused copy keys `visit.cta`, `tour.cta`). Navigation links, the owners' invitation line ("Experience La Jolla in person…"), the tour's email path, "Inquire about The Lexington", and the planning, privacy and terms pages are unchanged.
4. Without the page script the link simply floats at the bottom and the empty slots are not shown. The button is at least 48px tall, keyboard-focusable (it comes right after the header in the tab order) with a visible focus ring, hidden while the booking dialog is open and not printed.

Occasion cards no longer contain a link, so the v1.29.0 behaviour "a card holding keyboard focus keeps the frame" no longer applies; pointer and reading-follow behaviour are unchanged.

Tests: new `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` (one visit link, its three slots, touch size, reduced motion, dialog). Updated `opening`, `tour`, and the browser suites (`public-foundation`, `review`, `landing-motion`, `immersive`, `occasion-photos`) to use the single button, check it stays in the viewport, at least 44px tall, with no transition under reduced motion, and that occasion cards carry no links.

Review evidence (model, effort, exact head, findings and resolutions) is recorded in the feature pull request and its release pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: no tracking existed for the old buttons, so none was added. On very short phones the very bottom of the footer scrolls the docked button away with "Let's begin" (it is never drawn over the footer). No real-device (iPhone/iPad) check was possible from the build environment.

Next useful improvement: measure tour bookings from the single button once the owner chooses an analytics tool.
