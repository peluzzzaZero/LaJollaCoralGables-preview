# v1.31.0 — sharper event photographs in AVIF, three new ones placed

On 20 October 2026 the owner sent less-compressed copies of the event photographs placed in v1.28.0 and v1.29.0, plus three new photographs from the same events, and asked that they be served in a modern format that keeps the quality with little weight.

1. Every event photograph (first dance, set table, album 03 and 04, the five "One hall, many moods" cards and the occasion frames) is rebuilt by the new `scripts/event-photos.py` from the new originals: same crops as before, converted to sRGB, no metadata. Each is served as AVIF q62 through `<picture>` with a WebP q82 fallback for browsers without AVIF. The `<picture>` wrapper uses `display: contents`, so layout, sticky frame, hover effects and lazy loading are unchanged. The 13 replaced files weigh 703 KB as AVIF against 759 KB for the previous WebP files (about 7% less), and are encoded from less-compressed sources; in the before/after crops the gradients and foliage show less blocking. The build is reproducible (single-threaded AVIF, untagged sRGB), and CI runs `python scripts/event-photos.py --check`. Every record carries the owner's approval.
2. The mosaic tiles are rebuilt by `scripts/social-tiles.py` from the new sources; those sources in `assets/venue/` are now the owner's originals with metadata removed (the two letterboxed receptions are cropped and saved at JPEG q95).
3. The three new photographs replace pictures that appeared twice on the landing: a rose centerpiece on a gold stand now illustrates Rentals & event services ("Tabletop, glassware and florals"); a white orchid reception illustrates Galas & fundraisers; green orchids on a black table illustrate "Something completely different?". New EN/ES alt text describes each one.

Provenance: `tests/VENUE-MEDIA.md` (new section for this delivery) and `assets/venue/web/event-photos.json`, which records every original's and delivered file's SHA-256 and treatment. `tests/events.test.mjs` checks the record, that every rebuilt WebP is always paired with its AVIF source, and that the retired bar photograph is never used.

Verification before the release pull request: `npm test`; local Chromium browser suites (occasion photos, social, desktop review, responsive review, public foundation, landing flow); before/after screenshots at 390 and 1440 px of the occasions, moods, services, album and mosaic, and a 200% crop comparison. WebKit runs in CI.

Limitations: the delivered copies are still about 804 px wide (1206 px for the bougainvillea tower), the same pixel size as before; quality improves through lower compression and the new format, not through resolution. No real-device check from the build environment.

Next: ask the owner for the camera originals, then add 1.5×/2× widths to the largest frames.
