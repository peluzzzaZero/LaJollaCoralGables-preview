# v1.36.1 — the first message in the chat is never lost

On 29 October 2026, on the live site, the first tap or message sometimes did nothing. It happened right after the chat opened or after "Start over", and the visitor had to send it again (seen three times in a normal Chrome).

1. `chat.js` is the assistant's widget at commit 9f56e01. The cause: a message or tap sent while the welcome, or the person check, was still on its way was ignored. Now it is kept and sent right after. "Start over" clears anything still waiting.
2. `tests/browser/chat.py` checks this with a slow welcome: a question typed at once is sent right after it, and it appears in the conversation. `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` pins the new copy by hash.

Verification: `npm run build && npm test`. The Chromium browser suites run locally, and CI also runs WebKit. The external review evidence is on the pull request.

Order: widget only. The assistant needs no change (lajollaAsistente acd8702 is live).
