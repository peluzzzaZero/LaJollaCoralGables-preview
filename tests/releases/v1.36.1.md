# v1.36.1 — the first message in the chat is never lost

On 29 October 2026, on the live site, the first tap or message sometimes did nothing. It happened right after the chat opened or after "Start over", and the visitor had to send it again (seen three times in a normal Chrome).

1. `chat.js` is the assistant's widget at commit c375de2. The cause: a message or tap sent while the welcome, or the person check, was still on its way was ignored. Now it shows in the conversation at once and is sent right after, and several are sent in order. "Start over" clears anything still waiting.
2. `tests/browser/chat.py` checks this with a slow welcome: two questions typed at once appear in the conversation immediately and are sent after it in order; "Start over" drops anything waiting. `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` pins the new copy by hash.

Verification: `npm run build && npm test`. The Chromium browser suites run locally, and CI also runs WebKit. The external review evidence is on the pull request.

Limitations: a waiting message shows above the welcome reply, because it was written before the welcome arrived. If the welcome fails, the waiting messages are still sent and answered one by one.
