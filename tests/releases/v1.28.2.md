# v1.28.2 — guest reviews across the full width

The owner reported that the "Guest experiences" block looked broken on a ~1900px desktop: the heading column left a large empty area, the third excerpt card was cut at the right edge, the browser's native scrollbar showed under the cards, and the Google invitation was a tall panel with mostly empty space.

1. The heading and intro sit above the reviews instead of in a narrow left column; the excerpts use the full content width.
2. The excerpt row shows whole cards only: three on desktop (≥1200px), two on tablet, one on phones, sized from the row width so no card is cut. Swipe, trackpad and keyboard scrolling still work.
3. Own controls (previous/next, translated labels, `aria-controls`, `aria-disabled` at the ends) and a slim progress line replace the native scrollbar, which is hidden only once the script runs; without script the native scroll remains.
4. The Google invitation is a single compact strip (label · text · actions) on desktop and stacks on tablet/phone, with a full-width button on phones.
5. When live Google reviews load (future key), they lay out three/two/one per row, and the excerpt note and controls leave with the excerpts.

The five owner-approved excerpts, names and occasions are unchanged (tests/REVIEW-EXCERPTS.md); no stars, aggregate rating or new reviews.

Verification: static suite, `tests/browser/social.py` (new assertions: no native scrollbar, no cut card, next control moves the row, note/controls hidden with Google reviews), landing-flow, desktop and public-foundation browser checks, before/after screenshots at 1920, 1440, 1280, 884, 390 and 320 in EN/ES, external Sonnet/high review of the exact head recorded in the pull requests.

Next useful improvement: real-iPhone check of the swipe and the controls.
