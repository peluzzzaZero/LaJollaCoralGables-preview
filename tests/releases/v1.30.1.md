# v1.30.1 — no arrows on links

After v1.29.2 replaced the "↗" characters with drawn arrows, the owners repeated that they do not want arrows on the site at all. This release removes every arrow decoration from links.

1. Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest cards: the arrow is replaced by "Follow" / "Seguir" in small caps (11px Manrope, .18em tracking, brand brown, 1px underline), visible on touch screens without hover; it darkens on hover and focus. Each card announces "(opens in a new tab)" / "(se abre en una pestaña nueva)" to screen readers.
2. "See more on Instagram", "Discover the house", and the single floating "Schedule a visit" (v1.30.0 removed the per-occasion links and the venue invitation): plain text, no arrow. "See more on Instagram" also announces the new tab.
3. Removed CSS: `.icon-arrow` and every `.link-arrow` drawing, hover nudge and transition.
4. Kept: the review carousel's previous/next chevrons (controls approved by the owner), the decorative flourish and the accordion "+" toggles.

Tests: `tests/arrows.test.mjs` keeps the emoji-capable character ban and now also fails on any `link-arrow` / `icon-arrow` in pages, scripts or styles, on any inline SVG other than the carousel chevrons, the flourish and the visit seal ring, and checks the three "Follow"/"Seguir" labels and the new-tab text in both languages. `tests/social.test.mjs` expects the new card markup.

Verification before the release pull request: `npm test` (155 passing). Local Chromium screenshots of the social cards at 320 px (no horizontal overflow, label inside the card) and, at 390 px (mobile emulation) and 1440 px in EN/ES of the social cards, Instagram handle, occasion link, arrival hint, venue invitation and "Open in Google Maps"; mobile WebKit runs in CI.

Review evidence is recorded in the feature pull request and its release pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: no real-iPhone check from the build environment.
