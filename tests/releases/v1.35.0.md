# v1.35.0 — the chat's first question fits the page; the seal steps back while reading on phones

Two items from the owners' assistant guide (Epic Lux, 20 Oct 2026), section 3A, "the chatbot on the page".

1. **Opening question by section.** When the visitor opens the chat with the seal while The Lexington (`#alcazar`) is across the middle of the screen, the chat opens with "Upstairs, The Lexington is our private lounge… Planning something intimate upstairs?" With the occasions (`#events`) in view, it asks "What are we celebrating?". Anywhere else the welcome is unchanged. `script.js` passes `{ section }` to `LaJollaChat.open()`; the assistant (lajollaAsistente e46263c) chooses the text and starts the event plan with the answer.
2. **The seal does not cover the text on phones.** At 900 px and below, while it floats, the seal shrinks into its corner as the visitor scrolls down (scale .75: 48 px, still a full tap target; never hidden, no opacity or clip change) and returns when they scroll up or pause for 0.9 s. It shrinks around its own corner on screen, and only once fully formed; a wobble under 24 px, the rubber band past either end of the page, or a jump to a section does not flip it. With reduced motion it keeps its size.
3. `chat.js` is the assistant's widget at commit e46263c (adds the optional `section` to the first message); `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` pins it by hash.

Verification: `npm run build && npm test`; Chromium browser suites run locally with `LAJOLLA_WEB_ROOT=$PWD/dist`; `tests/browser/landing-motion.py` now checks at 390 px that the seal shrinks in its own corner, never below 48 px, comes back after a pause and keeps its size with reduced motion; screenshots at 390 px with the seal compact and restored, and the chat opened from The Lexington and from the occasions; external review (Claude CLI, Sonnet/high) of the PR head.

Order: the assistant change is backwards compatible in both directions (an older widget sends no section; an older worker reads only `sessionId`, `message`, `lang` and `turnstile` and ignores `section`), and is deployed to Cloudflare before or with this release.

Limitations: La Maison du Thé has no section of its own on the page yet, so it has no opening question. The section is the one that crosses the middle line of the screen when the seal is tapped.
