# v1.35.0 — the chat's first question fits the page; the seal steps back while reading on phones; short links in the chat

Two items from the owners' assistant guide (Epic Lux, 20 Oct 2026), section 3A, "the chatbot on the page".

1. **Opening question by section.** When the visitor opens the chat with the seal while The Lexington (`#alcazar`) is across the middle of the screen, the chat opens with "Upstairs, The Lexington is our private lounge… Planning something intimate upstairs?" With the occasions (`#events`) in view, it asks "What are we celebrating?". Anywhere else the welcome is unchanged. `script.js` passes `{ section }` to `LaJollaChat.open()`; the assistant (lajollaAsistente e46263c and later) chooses the text and starts the event plan with the answer.
2. **The seal does not cover the text on phones.** At 900 px and below, while it floats, the seal shrinks into its corner as the visitor scrolls down (scale .75: 48 px, still a full tap target; never hidden, no opacity or clip change) and returns when they scroll up or pause for 0.9 s. It shrinks around its own corner on screen, and only once fully formed; a wobble under 24 px or the rubber band past either end of the page does not flip it. (A smooth scroll after an in-page menu link can shrink it until the pause.) With reduced motion it keeps its size.
3. **Short, clickable links in the chat** (Dan, 29 Oct: the reviews answer showed a long raw Google Maps address that could not be tapped). Addresses in the assistant's text are never shown raw: "Instagram (https://…)" shows the linked word "Instagram", a bare address shows a short label ("Read our Google reviews", "Get directions", the booking calendar, or the domain), the phone is tap-to-call and info@ a mail link. A word of the text names a link only for the venue's own pages (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Google Maps, the site, Bookings); any other address shows its real domain. Links open in a new tab; the booking calendar opens the site's own Bookings window. This also fixes the text of the assistant version live today.
4. `chat.js` is the assistant's widget at commit 2d3364f (the optional `section` on the first message, and the links); `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` pins it by hash.

Verification: `npm run build && npm test`; Chromium browser suites run locally with `LAJOLLA_WEB_ROOT=$PWD/dist`; `tests/browser/landing-motion.py` now checks at 390 px that the seal shrinks in its own corner, never below 48 px, comes back after a pause and keeps its size with reduced motion; `tests/browser/chat.py` checks the short links and that the section reaches the first message. Screenshots shared with the owner outside the repo: the seal compact and restored at 390 px, the chat opened from The Lexington and from the occasions, and the reviews and social answers with short links (EN/ES). The external review evidence lives on the PR, not in this file.

Order: the assistant change is backwards compatible in both directions (an older widget sends no section; an older worker reads only `sessionId`, `message`, `lang` and `turnstile` and ignores `section`), and is deployed to Cloudflare before or with this release.

Limitations: La Maison du Thé has no section of its own on the page yet, so it has no opening question. The section is the one that crosses the middle line of the screen when the seal is tapped. The section question only opens a new conversation: a visitor with an earlier chat in this browser keeps that conversation, and "Start over" gives the general welcome.
