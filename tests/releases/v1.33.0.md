# v1.33.0 — the "Imagine it your way" board comes alive, and three visual fixes

Dan saw v1.32.0 live. He liked the board but found it static ("esta bonito si y me gusta pero estatico... ponle como algun efecto que combine con el sitio y la imagen"), and sent screenshots of three places with a problem ("estas 3 estan con problema"): the "Good to know" card with the floor plan, the florals photograph in Services and The Lexington.

## The board moves

1. **Arrival with the scroll**, driven by the board's own `--board-arrival` (set by `script.js` like the other scroll scenes: 0 as the board enters the view, 1 once its middle reaches the middle of the view), so it plays forwards and backwards. From 901 px the split closes: the facade slides in from the left and the salon from the right with a slight tilt, the LJ seal stamps the seam (it grows from half size and turns into place) and the plaque polaroid drops onto the corner. Up to 900 px the three polaroids are dealt onto the card from three directions.
2. **Depth**: the facade and the salon drift gently inside their frames, like the history and hall photographs (`data-landing-parallax`).
3. **On a desktop pointer**: the picture under the pointer zooms slightly while the other one softens, its label lifts, the seal turns once, and the plaque straightens and comes forward.

Nothing moves under reduced motion or Save-Data: the arrival and drift rules sit under `.landing-effects`, which `script.js` never sets in those cases, and the hover rules need `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. No `opacity: 0` and no `clip-path`; pictures, text and labels are always visible.

## Fixes

4. **Floor plan ("Good to know")**: the labels sat at the card edges, far from their drawings, with a wide empty gap between the floors. `scripts/extra-photos.py` now sets both floors side by side at the same scale with a 140 px gap (1945×1460 instead of 2100×1460; the drawing itself is unchanged), and each label is centred under its own floor through a caption grid that follows the image's proportions.
5. **Florals photograph (Services)**: the bouquet was cut at the top and the caption touched the left edge of its plate. It is now a framed print (12 px border, padded caption) showing the whole bouquet (taller limit, framing from the top).
6. **The Lexington**: the picture sat inside a dark gap with a second line around it, so it looked smaller than its window. It now fills the arch with one gold hairline and a soft halo, and the arch is a little lower so less of the room is cut.

Version: minor, because the board gains new scroll and hover motion.

## Verification

`npm test`, `python scripts/extra-photos.py --check` and all 11 browser suites, run locally on Chromium. Before/after screenshots of the three fixes at 1440 and 390 px, and the board mid-arrival and settled at 1440 and 390 px; the external Sonnet/high review is recorded on the pull request.

## Limitations and next

WebKit (Safari) was not run locally; CI runs it. On phones under about 360 px, "Second floor" / "Planta alta" is wider than its column and stays centred by overflowing both sides slightly. Next: a smaller default size for the plan with the full drawing on tap, and a 1.5×/2× facade crop if the owners have a larger file.
