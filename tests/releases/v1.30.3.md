# v1.30.3 — the visit invitation becomes a closing line

The owner did not like the block under the venue walk on desktop: since v1.30.0 removed its button, the sentence "Experience La Jolla in person…" sat alone on the left of a full-width cream band with empty space on the right.

1. The band, its borders and its pointer light are gone. The owner's sentence (unchanged, EN and ES) is now a centred closing line in the display serif, in italic, under a short hairline in the brand brown, as a quiet close to the venue walk. No button was added: "Schedule a visit" stays the single floating seal.
2. On phones (≤650 px) the line is drawn up into the empty room under the pinned venue-walk photo only while the walk is active (`.venue-walk.is-walking`); when the walk is collapsed (reduced motion, Save-Data, small screens, failed film) it keeps the normal spacing so it never overlaps the hall caption.
3. The pointer-light effect no longer targets `.visit-invitation` (`script.js`), since the line is no longer a card.

Tests: `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` checks the owner sentence, the centred line without background or pointer light, and the walking-only mobile pull-up.

Verification: `npm test`; screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844 in EN and ES with the walk active, plus 320 and 390 px under reduced motion (collapsed walk) in EN and ES, inspected; the desktop and phone captures were shared with the owner.

Review evidence is recorded in the feature pull request and its release pull request, not in this file.

Limitations: no real-device check from the build environment.

Next useful improvement: a browser assertion that the invitation never intersects the hall caption at 320/390 px in both walk modes.
