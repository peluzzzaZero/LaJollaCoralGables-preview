# v1.34.0 review

External review with the Claude CLI (`scripts/review-feature.py`, Sonnet, high effort), against `develop` at d8d344d.

## Round 1: 8c1e0d1, blocked

One blocking finding: the disclosures and the release record did not agree.

- The roadmap still listed the hand-off and team alert as "Next", while the release notes said they were live.
- The chat did not say that it is an AI assistant; the only notice was "We keep this conversation to help you."
- The privacy page's claims about the assistant could not be checked from this repository.

What changed:

- **AI disclosure in the chat.** Header: "AI assistant of the La Jolla team" / "Asistente con IA del equipo de La Jolla". Note under the message box: replies are written by AI, and what the visitor types is processed by AI services and kept to help them. Both are checked by `tests/visit-cta.test.mjs` and `tests/browser/chat.py`.
- **Roadmap and notes agree.** The hand-off and the team email are live. A test request reached the info@ inbox on 28 Oct 2026, through the Entra app "La Jolla Assistant" (Mail.Send). The README sentence about inbox delivery is about the release workflow, which still does not check inboxes.
- **Privacy page.** It now says the seal opens the chat on the home page, which is the only page that loads `chat.js`.
- **Evidence for the privacy claims.** All of it is in `lajollaAsistente` at commit 138b4fa:
  - 30-day deletion: `src/server/worker.ts` (daily cron prunes leads, and the D1 checkpointer keeps 30 days).
  - Phone numbers and emails removed before a model: `src/llm/pii.ts`.
  - Providers: `wrangler.toml` (`LLM_CHAIN = "workers-ai,nvidia"`).
  - Team email: `src/leads/notify.ts`.
  - The NVIDIA sentence ("may use the text it receives to improve its models") is deliberately the cautious case for a free hosted trial service.

Non-blocking findings fixed in the widget (commit 138b4fa):

- A request that hangs ends after 20 seconds with the calendar link, and "Start over" cancels a waiting request.
- Only https, tel and mailto links become buttons.
- A saved item without reply text is skipped (`render()` returns early).
- On phones, the field is not focused on open (the keyboard no longer covers the welcome), and the sheet respects safe areas.
- The typing indicator has `role="status"`.
- The public copy has no internal notes.
- The new browser test `tests/browser/chat.py` covers these cases, and screenshots were taken at 390, 884 and 1440 px (kept outside the repository).

Left for later (non-blocking):

- A focus trap and scroll lock while the phone sheet is open.
- The seal's `aria-controls` still names the booking window.
- Vendoring `chat.js` from the assistant repository by hand.

## Round 2: 330ac06, blocked

One blocking finding: the release notes claimed verification that the repository does not perform. They said that CI runs the chat on WebKit, but it does not. They also cited 884 px screenshots that are not in the repository.

What changed:

- **The notes say what was tested.** The chat was tested on Chromium only, and a real-iPhone check is the first follow-up. The screenshots are kept outside the repository.
- **Widget, commit 4e50395:**
  - An expired saved chat is removed from the browser, matching the 30-day sentence on the privacy page.
  - On phones, "Start over" no longer raises the keyboard either. Focus goes to the chat dialog itself, so screen readers stay inside it.
- **Vendoring.** `chat.js` must match the SHA-256 of the upstream file at 4e50395 (`tests/visit-cta.test.mjs`).

Left for later (non-blocking):

- iOS keyboard handling (`visualViewport`) and landscape phones.
- The Node preview server does not serve `chat.js` or `social.js`, so `native-forms.py` and `bookings.py` exercise the calendar fallback.
- More chat cases: a reload that keeps the conversation, tapping a quick reply, a late answer after "Start over", and a tap before `chat.js` loads.
- The `:has()` rule that hides the seal while the chat is open.
