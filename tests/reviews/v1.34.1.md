## Round 1: 6114d27, blocked

**Blocking finding.** The widget stopped its 20-second limit as soon as the assistant asked for the check. A Turnstile script that stalled, or a check that never called back, would therefore have left the chat waiting forever.

What changed (widget commit 5976f4a, copied as `chat.js`):

- The 20-second limit now covers loading Turnstile and the invisible check. It pauses only when Turnstile asks the visitor for a tap (`before-interactive-callback`).
- The timeout, unsupported, error and expired callbacks all end with the calendar help.
- A Turnstile script that loads without `window.turnstile` can be tried again on the next message.
- "Start over" removes the check in progress, and a late answer from the old check is ignored.
- The check takes no room while it runs invisibly. A check that needs a tap uses the compact size below 420 px.

**Non-blocking findings fixed:**

- `tests/browser/chat.py` now covers a check that never answers, a script that never loads, a check that needs a tap at 390 px (fit, no time limit), and "Start over" during the check.
- The release notes cite the assistant commits for every claim about that repository and label those claims as reported from there. They also reconcile the 50, 54 and 102 conversation counts and record the test results.
- The notes no longer say the hash proves the flow. The test now also checks `interaction-only` and the five callbacks.
