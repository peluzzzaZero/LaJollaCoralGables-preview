# Scroll World + Elaya adaptation

The user requested both skills, then clarified that the entire direction needed professional critique rather than a return to the earlier font. Their building, original films and content remain authoritative. Scroll World contributes endpoint-preserving camera pacing, lazy blob delivery, coalesced seeks and mobile priming. Elaya contributes one typeface, a clear hierarchy/action, factual copy and complete interaction states.

The owner’s feedback supersedes the prior serif/script retention and repeated word masks. Version 1.15.0 uses a self-hosted Manrope variable font with approved logo imagery, complete text, meaningful original room evidence and two restrained photographic assembly moments. The one full-screen timeline contains all nine chapters; planning stays separate. The adaptive camera selects a continuous approved panorama on wide screens and native recorded sequences on phones, with a later excerpt replacing reversed repetition. No new generation spend or architectural reconstruction. See CINEMATIC-ART-DIRECTION.md and the art-direction source manifest.

Historical v1.14.0 produced three five-second Venice Seedance Mini generations quoted at 0.47 USD each. v1.14.1 removed the distorted ballroom take from the landing. Historical media and original room photos remain unchanged. This implementation does not claim that independent views constitute a measured physical camera route into each room.
